Who We Are

Based on the Setswana word for “dawn,” Gosele Consulting was established in 2007 to solve business challenges and help create new beginnings for the organisations it serves.

Our services span skills development and accredited training, occupational health and safety compliance, social and stakeholder facilitation, and ISO management systems.
